# Comments, More from Fabris, and home cards

- Nicknames are gone. Comments show the account name, or "Guest 4f2a" (first four characters of the guest id) for guests. Names already saved on old comments are unchanged.
- The comment box is a rounded chat-style field with a round send button on its right. It grows as you type. Tapping the comment button only opens the comments; it never opens the keyboard by itself.
- "More from Fabris" sits at the bottom of the poem page: the latest four other poems in two columns, thumbnail and name only.
- The home page shows each poem as a thumbnail, its name and its published date. No stats, kind or duration.

No SQL and no Worker change. Just deploy the site files.
